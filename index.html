Data Backup (JSON) & Storage Info

Storage Architecture: This application automatically saves all your edits instantly using localStorage. This means your data will persist across page refreshes, closing tabs, and browser restarts. However, there are limitations:
- Data is bound exclusively to this specific browser on this specific device. It will not automatically sync to your phone or another laptop.
- LocalStorage has a size limit (typically 5MB), which is more than enough for textual study logs, but you should still keep backups.
- Clearing your browser's site data or cache will erase this data.

Cross-device Access: Automatic cloud sync is not supported in this offline version. To move your progress to another device, you must Export your backup here and Import it on the other device.

Use the buttons below to manually export a secure JSON backup of your progress or to restore a previous session.
